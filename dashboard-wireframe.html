Dashboard Wireframe

A branded command-center view for monitoring prior authorization risk, trust failure events, reconciliation density, patient access risk, and clean claim readiness.

Dashboard Purpose

The PARCS dashboard is designed to show which prior authorization cases are most likely to break, why they are at risk, who owns the next action, and what downstream damage may occur if the risk is not resolved.

This dashboard should not feel like a generic task board. It should feel like a healthcare operations command view.

Executive Read

Operational interpretation

The dashboard should detect instability before the patient, claim, or staff member absorbs the impact. Critical cases, trust failures, documentation defects, and reconciliation density are surfaced early so leaders can intervene before delays become denials.

Dashboard Sections

Executive KPI RowShows immediate workflow health: active cases, critical risk, access risk, reconciliation density, and clean claim readiness.
Risk Triage QueueRanks cases by urgency so staff are not relying on inbox order or memory.

Operational Drift MonitorDetects small inconsistencies before they compound into backlog.
Reconciliation Density PanelExposes hidden manual work keeping the workflow alive.
Clean Claim Readiness GateConnects authorization approval to downstream claim protection.

Executive Design Rule

The dashboard should answer: What is breaking, why is it breaking, who owns the fix, how urgent is it, what downstream risk does it create, and what metric proves improvement?
