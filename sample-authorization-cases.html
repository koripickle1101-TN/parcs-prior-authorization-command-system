Dataset Purpose

This sample dataset gives PARCS a realistic operating base. It shows how prior authorization cases can be reviewed by service date, payer, plan type, documentation readiness, case status, patient access risk, denial risk, clean claim readiness, and final outcome.

The data is synthetic and created for portfolio demonstration purposes. It is not patient data and does not contain protected health information.
