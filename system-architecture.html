System Architecture

A layered healthcare operations control system designed to reduce uncertainty, improve visibility, and prevent prior authorization defects from moving downstream into patient access disruption or denial risk.

Control Layer Purpose

LayerPurpose
Authorization TrackerCreates one source of truth for case status, owner, risk, and next action.
Documentation Readiness GatePrevents submission before required clinical support is ready.
Payer Rule LibraryConverts tribal payer knowledge into documented workflow intelligence.
Routing Decision TreeRemoves confusion about where exceptions go next.
Risk Scoring EnginePrioritizes cases most likely to fail.
Clean Claim Readiness GateConfirms authorization is ready for downstream claim submission.

Design Standard

PARCS should make workflow risk visible before the patient, staff member, or claim absorbs the damage.
