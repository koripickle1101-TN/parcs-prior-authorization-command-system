# Failure Cascade Map

## PARCS 2.0

Created by Kori Pickle

## Purpose

This map shows how one small prior authorization issue can create downstream workflow damage. The goal is to show risk before it becomes patient access disruption, denial risk, or staff burden.

## Cascade 1: Trust Collapse

```text
Eligibility conflict
↓
Manual payer verification begins
↓
Side notes or email updates appear
↓
Duplicate workflow emerges
↓
Routing confidence drops
↓
Escalations increase
↓
Turnaround time drifts
↓
Reconciliation workload expands
↓
Patient access and revenue risk increase
```

## PARCS Response

```text
Eligibility conflict
↓
Trust failure event logged
↓
Case routed to verification owner
↓
Tracker updated
↓
Manual verification count captured
↓
Reconciliation reason assigned
↓
Escalation trigger applied
↓
Case remains visible
↓
Recovery happens earlier
```

## Cascade 2: Operational Drift

```text
Documentation delay
↓
Authorization submission slows
↓
Pending queue ages
↓
Payer timing variance adds uncertainty
↓
Staff shortage reduces follow-up capacity
↓
Shift turnover creates status gaps
↓
Rework increases
↓
Escalations become reactive
↓
Downstream claim risk increases
```

## PARCS Response

```text
Documentation delay
↓
Documentation readiness gate flags risk
↓
Case status changes to Documentation Pending
↓
Assigned owner receives next action
↓
Aging threshold tracks service date risk
↓
Escalation trigger activates before crisis
↓
Pending queue remains visible
↓
Day-before-service risk decreases
```

## Cascade 3: Human Dependency

```text
Experienced staff unavailable
↓
Payer-specific knowledge becomes harder to access
↓
Routing confusion increases
↓
Manual interpretation replaces standard work
↓
Authorization accuracy weakens
↓
Hidden reconciliation work surfaces
↓
Escalation volume increases
↓
Workflow continuity depends on who is working
```

## PARCS Response

```text
Experienced staff unavailable
↓
Payer rule library provides requirements
↓
Routing decision tree provides next step
↓
Authorization tracker shows case status
↓
Reconciliation reason codes show repair needs
↓
Escalation rules define support path
↓
Workflow continues with less dependence on one person
```

## Executive Interpretation

PARCS does not pretend that workflow defects disappear. It makes defects visible, assignable, measurable, and correctable before they create downstream damage.
