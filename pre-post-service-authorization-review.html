Operational lesson

A retro request is not the same thing as a resolved authorization.

When pre-service authorization is missing or unresolved, a new exception workflow begins. The student model requires the applicable payer/program source to be reviewed, ownership to be visible, evidence requirements to be identified, active movement to be documented, and closure to be verified.

Retro request submitted≠ authorization resolved
Missing prior authorization≠ automatic eligibility for post-service review
Technically open≠ actively moving
Submission≠ closure

Patient-to-professional perspective

The patient experiences the uncertainty after the service.

The operations team has to reconstruct whether the required control happened before service, what exception path is available now, which evidence is required, who owns resolution, and what proves the case is actually closed.

Career simulation boundary

What PARCS does not decide

PARCS does not determine medical necessity, coverage, coding accuracy, payer liability, contractual rights, legal obligations, or whether a real payer must permit retroactive or retrospective authorization. Those decisions require current authoritative guidance and the appropriate qualified role.
