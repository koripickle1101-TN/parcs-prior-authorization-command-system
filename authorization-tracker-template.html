Authorization Tracker Template

A source-of-truth tracker designed to make prior authorization status, ownership, trust failures, reconciliation work, patient access risk, denial risk, and clean claim readiness visible in one place.

Open CSV Template Back to Artifacts

Template Purpose

The authorization tracker is the operational center of PARCS 2.0. It prevents side spreadsheets, email-only status updates, verbal workarounds, and unclear case ownership by creating one official source of truth for prior authorization workflow status.

The tracker is designed to answer: What is the case status, who owns the next action, what risk exists, what manual repair work occurred, and whether the case is ready for downstream claim submission?

Control Rule

If a case is not visible in the tracker, it does not officially exist in the workflow.
