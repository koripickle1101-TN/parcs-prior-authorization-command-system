# Escalation Quality Score

## PARCS 2.0

**Created by Kori Pickle**

## Purpose

The Escalation Quality Score measures whether escalation prevented workflow damage or simply reacted after the damage was already visible.

## Scoring Factors

| Escalation Factor | Points |
|---|---:|
| Escalated before deadline | 25 |
| Clear owner assigned | 25 |
| Clear next action documented | 20 |
| Resolved before service date | 20 |
| Root cause coded | 10 |

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 80-100 | Strong escalation |
| 60-79 | Acceptable escalation |
| 40-59 | Weak escalation |
| Below 40 | Crisis escalation |

## Control Rule

Escalation success should be measured by prevention, not volume.

## Executive Use

More escalation is not automatically bad. Late, vague, repeated, or ownerless escalation is bad. Strong escalation is early, specific, owned, documented, and resolved before patient access or claim readiness is damaged.
