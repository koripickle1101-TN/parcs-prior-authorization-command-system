# Authorization Risk Scoring Model

## PARCS 2.0

**Created by Kori Pickle**

## Purpose

The Authorization Risk Score ranks cases by likelihood of workflow failure. The score helps staff prioritize the cases most likely to disrupt patient access, increase denial risk, or require manual reconciliation.

## Scoring Factors

| Risk Factor | Points |
|---|---:|
| Service date within 3 business days | 25 |
| Documentation not ready | 20 |
| Payer response overdue | 20 |
| Eligibility conflict | 15 |
| Demographic mismatch | 15 |
| Prior denial history for same payer or service | 15 |
| Missing owner | 20 |
| Manual verification already required | 10 |
| High-dollar or high-impact service | 10 |
| Staff shortage day | 10 |

## Risk Levels

| Score | Level | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-20 | Low | Normal workflow |
| 21-40 | Moderate | Monitor during daily review |
| 41-60 | High | Same-day review |
| 61+ | Critical | Immediate escalation |

## Control Rule

No case should reach the day before service without a visible risk score.

## Executive Use

The risk score turns authorization work from first-in-first-out processing into risk-based operational triage.
