Test 2: Operational Drift

Determines whether small workflow inconsistencies compound into queue buildup, rework, delayed reimbursement, and operational drag.

Result: Moderate residual risk. Drift is not eliminated, but it becomes visible earlier and easier to contain.

Test 3: Human Dependency

Determines whether the workflow is truly integrated or dependent on invisible human middleware, institutional memory, and go-to problem solvers.

Result: Moderate residual risk. Human expertise still matters, but the workflow no longer depends entirely on one go-to person.

Executive Conclusion

PARCS 2.0 does not pretend workflow instability disappears. It makes instability visible, assignable, measurable, and correctable before it becomes patient disruption, denial risk, or staff burden.
