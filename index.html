01

What PARCS 2.0 Solves

PARCS reframes prior authorization delays as operational reliability failures caused by weak visibility, unclear ownership, documentation gaps, payer variation, and hidden manual reconciliation.

Trust Failure Detection

Captures moments when staff stop trusting eligibility, payer status, demographics, routing, or documentation timing.

Risk-Based Triage

Ranks authorization cases by urgency so staff can act on the cases most likely to break first.

Clean Claim Readiness

Connects authorization approval to downstream claim readiness so defects do not move into billing.
