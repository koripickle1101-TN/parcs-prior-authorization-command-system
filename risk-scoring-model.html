Authorization Risk Scoring Model

A risk-based triage model that ranks authorization cases by likelihood of workflow failure, patient access disruption, denial exposure, or hidden manual reconciliation.

Scoring Factors

Risk FactorPoints
Service date within 3 business days25
Documentation not ready20
Payer response overdue20
Eligibility conflict15
Demographic mismatch15
Prior denial history for same payer or service15
Missing owner20
Manual verification already required10

Risk Levels

ScoreLevelAction
0-20LowNormal workflow
21-40ModerateMonitor during daily review
41-60HighSame-day review
61+CriticalImmediate escalation

Control Rule

No case should reach the day before service without a visible risk score.
